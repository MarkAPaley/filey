Privacy Policy

filey (this “App”) does not transmit information to anyone, other than
making requests for web pages from www.epguides.com .

It does not even print your name (although your OS username might be
stored in the INI file as part of your directory location
configuration).

The App reads a text file and web pages from www.epguides.com , for TV
series that you select, based upon names of files that you drop onto the
App.

The App creates some working dBase DBF files, which it uses to lookup
standardized names for those files, and then offers to change your
filenames into the new standardized formats.
